# TCEHY — Video Talking Points

- Tencent is the company behind WeChat — the app over a billion people in China use every day to message, pay, read news, and run their small businesses.
- Open. A wide-moat cash machine at a fair-to-cheap multiple with modest downside and multiple-normalization upside deserves capital as a monitored satellite — but the payoff, not the business, is the bet. The verdict flips to Watchlist or Close if FY2026 FCF falls below ~RMB 190B while capex climbs, or if the discount to 0700.HK widens through two quarters of rising earnings.
- Moat: WeChat's billion-user network and near-total switching costs are one of the world's widest consumer moats; held below elite only because ByteDance contests the ad growth engine.
- Risk: Operating margin (33%) is still 15 points below its FY2021 peak (48%) even as gross margin hit a record — opex is where AI spend is hiding.
- Verdict: open. Dead Simple Score 93/100.
