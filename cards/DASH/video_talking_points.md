# DASH — Video Talking Points

- DoorDash runs the app you open for food — and now groceries, alcohol, and retail.
- At 41x free cash flow it trades 28% above its own base case, priced for two unproven bets to both win.
- The bull case: a dominant restaurant duopoly that prints cash, with a near-100%-margin ad engine on top that lifts blended margins mechanically — even if grocery never works.
- The main risk: EPS fell four straight quarters and the company won't disclose the restaurant-versus-grocery split that resolves the whole thesis.
- Verdict: ownable business, real moat, wrong price — watch for Aug 4 to show stable restaurant margin and 20%+ ad growth.
- Let the feared bad quarter be the entry where the asymmetry finally points up.
