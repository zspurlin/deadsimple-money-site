# ELF — Video Talking Points

- e.l.f. makes cheap, on-trend makeup, skincare, and now hair care for younger shoppers.
- It's beaten earnings four quarters running while expanding into hair care and overseas.
- The bull point: you're paying a normal ~20x price for an above-normal 20%-plus grower with a structural cost edge.
- The main risk: the whole thesis rests on growth not slowing, and the earnings beats are already shrinking.
- There's $627M of net debt that turns toxic if growth stalls and the cash never converts.
- Verdict: a small satellite buy with a clean kill-switch on the Aug 4 print — trim hard if growth disappoints.
