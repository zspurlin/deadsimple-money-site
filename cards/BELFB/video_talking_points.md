# BELFB — Video Talking Points

- Bel Fuse makes the small, unglamorous electronic parts that live inside bigger machines: fuses, circuit protectors, connectors, magnetics, and power modules.
- Watchlist. The business is genuinely good and one bear fear is partially falsified, but the price already assumes a durability the July 29 print hasn't confirmed. What flips it to Open: gross margin holding at/above ~38% with continued double-digit growth and structural-mix commentary — plus confirmation that 12.1M is the full share base.
- Moat: Genuine design-in switching costs in defense/aero, but narrow, unsized, and surrounded by commodity revenue where larger rivals win on scale.
- Risk: Gross margin durability is undocumented; a slip below ~35% or a "pricing" explanation would break the compounder case.
- Verdict: watchlist. Dead Simple Score 80/100.
