# NFLX — Video Talking Points

- Netflix is the streaming service people pay a monthly fee to watch movies and shows on.
- Next earnings land July 16, and that print tests whether the market's bet is intact.
- You're buying a $12B cash machine — the deepest content library on earth — at a fair price, with the ad business as a free option.
- But Netflix has missed EPS estimates four quarters running, twice by double digits — content costs may be rising faster than revenue.
- This isn't a pricing story; it's a retention-cost story — the moat protects how cheaply Netflix keeps you, not the price.
- Verdict: watch, leaning buy on the engine — buy the second half if margin holds flat-or-up on growing revenue, walk if it falls again.
