# DPZ — Video Talking Points

- Domino's operates a global pizza delivery franchise system, collecting royalties from franchisees rather than running restaurants directly.
- Recent earnings misses and $4.90 billion in net debt have pushed the stock near its bear case, creating potential opportunity.
- The franchise model provides scale advantages and insulates from labor costs while generating predictable royalty streams.
- At 7.5x net debt to free cash flow, there's no cushion if franchisees start failing under competitive pressure.
- The risk-reward is compelling at current prices, but the debt load demands proof franchisee economics can hold up.
- This is a leveraged bet on whether franchise royalties can survive the shift to third-party delivery platforms.
