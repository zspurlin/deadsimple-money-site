# TOST — Video Talking Points

- Toast is the technology a restaurant runs on — payments, ordering, kitchen displays, payroll, online orders, all in one system.
- Why now: it's profitable, earnings have beaten estimates four quarters running with accelerating beats, and a former bear just flipped.
- The bull point: every module a restaurant adds makes leaving harder, so the moat deepens as customers buy more.
- The main risk: most revenue is thin-margin payment processing, and the per-check take rate is contestable if Square starts a price war.
- The verdict: watch and buy a starter — the asymmetry is real and the catalyst is nine weeks out.
- August 3 confirms or kills it: revenue up 20%+ with EPS, size up; single-digit revenue or dilution over 5%, walk.
